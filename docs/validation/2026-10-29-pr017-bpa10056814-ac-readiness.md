The Phase 1.5 hard Gate correctly rejects those 91 lost protected cycle corridors, including when the Bus map is otherwise identity. The result is `MULTI_VOLTAGE_QUOTIENT_STRUCTURALLY_INVALID` with `INVALID_REASON=PROTECTED_PARALLEL_CYCLE_MULTIPLICITY_LOST`. The measured times exclude PSDCase parsing, PyPSA projection, and the extra strategy preflight. BASE's topology stage removed 129 Bus at 115 kV, four at 230 kV, and two at 10.5 kV. ## Acceptance decision

The DC Gate, card inventory, PSDCase AC parse, supported-device projection, authoritative sidecar, and Phase 1.5 export/reload path ran. The fixed status sequence is `AC_ONLY_READINESS_PASS`, `PSDCASE_TO_PYPSA_STRUCTURAL_PROJECTION_PASS`, and `MULTI_VOLTAGE_QUOTIENT_STRUCTURALLY_INVALID`. The real Beijing model **does not pass final structural acceptance** on 0.2.0 because protected parallel-only cycle corridors are aggregated. The candidate Bus counts 1392, 1395, and 1525 are unaccepted. No physical fidelity, AC power flow, reactive-power, Ward, AFDPF-R, OPF, UC, or GNN conclusion is drawn. `PHYSICAL_FIDELITY_NOT_YET_EVALUATED` remains the release boundary.

Jilin has 277 source Line rows; no quotient was run for Jilin, so its target field is null. The complete source contains nine Beijing and five Jilin `L+` cards. The legacy aggregate JSON key `both_cycle_corridor_count` means **active parallel multiplicity on a simple-graph cycle**. It does not mean that `parallel_only_cycle` and `simple_graph_cycle` are both true; those predicates are mutually exclusive for a corridor. The field retains its existing name and numerical values in this PR.

### ADR-009 design inputs

1. Preserve independent circuit multiplicity and StableId provenance for every protected parallel-only corridor through quotient, export, reload, and branch mapping. Do not weaken `cycle_corridors`.
2. Define behavior for two, three, and four original circuits per corridor, including distinct impedance, length, current rating, and L+ terminal-shunt associations. A one-Line aggregate cannot silently claim to preserve their topology.
3. Keep `L+` as an extension of its exact `L ` parent and map each terminal shunt with its source association; never interpret `L+` as an independent AC branch.
4. Specify deterministic circuit-level target identity and a many-to-many-safe provenance contract before implementing any parallel-Line retention. The final Gate must use persisted, reloaded network and mappings.
5. Keep active-state, voltage-layer, transformer-interface, control, and source-immutability Gates. State remains structural; physical fidelity requires separate later evidence.
